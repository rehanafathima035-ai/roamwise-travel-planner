# 🗺️ RoadWise — Smart Travel Planner

**Plan smarter. Travel better. Explore more.** ✈️

RoadWise is a travel-planning application designed to help users organize trips, explore destinations, and build personalized travel itineraries in one place. It aims to make travel planning simpler, more convenient, and more enjoyable.

## ✨ Features

* 🧭 **Trip Planning** — Organize travel plans around your chosen destinations.
* 📍 **Destination Exploration** — Discover places you may want to visit.
* 🗓️ **Itinerary Management** — Structure your travel activities into a convenient schedule.
* 💰 **Budget Planning** — Keep track of your planned travel expenses.
* 🚗 **Travel Organization** — Keep important trip details organized in one place.
* 📱 **User-Friendly Interface** — Navigate your travel plans through a simple interface.

> Features may vary depending on the current implementation.
